Project Brief: The "Billion Dollar Club" Dashboard

Objective
Design a strategic executive dashboard to analyze the global landscape of "Unicorn" companies (startups valued over $1 billion). The dashboard must empower Senior Investors to identify market trends, geographic hubs, and capital efficiency.
Target Audience
Senior Partners & Venture Capitalists (VCs).
Data Source
Download the file in Google Classroom. The file is named Unicorn_Companies.csv

Part 1: The Executive Summary (KPIs)
The dashboard must answer these four "Big Number" questions instantly:
"What is the total value of our potential market?"
Metric: Total Market Cap (Sum of Valuation)
"How many opportunities (companies) are there currently?"
Metric: Unicorn Count (Count of Companies)
"What is the average price tag of a deal?"
Metric: Average Valuation
"How fast do these companies typically scale?"
Metric: Average Years to Unicorn Status

Part 2: The Strategic Questions (Visuals)
The dashboard must visualize the answers to these four strategic questions:
"Are we in a startup boom or a bust?"
Requirement: Visualize the history of new unicorns joining the club each year to show the trend.
"Which industries are creating the most massive wealth?"
Requirement: Rank the industries (e.g., Fintech, AI) by their total valuation and show the top 4 industries to see where the money is concentrated.
"Where are the global hotspots for innovation?"
Requirement: Map the valuation by country to identify the geographic centers of power.
"Who is efficient with capital, and who is just burning cash?"
Requirement: Analyze the relationship between Funding Raised vs. Current Valuation to spot the most efficient companies.

Part 3: The Interactive Capability (Filters)
The user must be able to "slice" the entire report to answer:
"Can I see all of the above insights specifically for Industry or Continent?"
Requirement: Interactive slicers for Industry (e.g., Artificial Intelligence) and Continent (e.g., Asia, North America).
